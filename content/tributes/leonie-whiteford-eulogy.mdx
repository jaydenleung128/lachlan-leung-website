Lachlan had a passion for Table Tennis. His brothers Matthew & Jayden also played and represented NSW, and Simon & Connie volunteer heavily for Table Tennis, both for their local clubs and for NSW.

Lachlan's representative Table Tennis journey begins as a nine-year-old, at the 2018 Australian Junior Championships in Townsville, where a test event was introduced for the U11 age group — what an exciting time this was for these young players. Lachlan formed many new friendships.

The 2019 Junior Championships were held in Wollongong, where the first official full event for the U11's was held. Lachlan's team won the first ever, official team **GOLD medal**.

The Table Tennis continued for Lachlan, where he played in teams for his local club and for NSW, with many of the same boys.

In 2023, Lachlan completed the TOPS & Level One coaching courses. At this course there was a new coach from overseas, and he brought new skills and techniques — this gave Lachlan a new enthusiasm for his table tennis, and his game continued to advance rapidly.

In 2024 these new skills and confidence were evident in his results:

- The 2024 TTA Tour event in ACT — U17 Boy's singles — **GOLD medal**
- The 2024 TTA Tour event in Mandurah — U17 Boy's singles — **GOLD medal**
- The 2024 Junior Championships in Mandurah — U17 Boys' Team — **GOLD medal**

These results were reflected in the TTA 2024 end of year ranking list. Lachlan was ranked the **number five player in Australia** for U17 Boy's.

Lachlan loved playing for his school at the annual Schoolboy's Cup & Shield events. When his school didn't progress, he was first to sign up for the repechage singles event. Lachlan won many of these events — he was so proud to win for his school. Lachlan was always respectful to his opponents, offering coaching advice to other players as well as to schoolteachers who had no background in table tennis. This was deeply appreciated and admired by all he came in contact with.

In 2025, Lachlan was determined to compete for his school at both these events.

- The Schoolboy's Cup — Lachlan's school won a medal for the first time, a **bronze medal**.
- The Schoolboy's Shield — Lachlan's school won the **GOLD medal**.

What an amazing effort. This was the most courageous thing I have ever had the privilege of witnessing. When you speak of highlights, highlights are not always results on paper — but the absolutely amazing efforts, in either winning or not winning. We take courage, strength, and fortitude in the real values of participating in the sport we love.

Lachlan's legacy will be forever ingrained in our memories, for his ethical integrity, his sense of fair play, and his kindness towards others. He will be remembered forever with love, and sadly missed by our community.

Rest in peace, Lachlan.
